FAQ

Why pay when some library and bookshop clubs are free?

Some are, and some are good. This is a different thing: six fixed Wednesdays, a reading list chosen for you by a librarian, and a drink in the foyer afterwards. It costs €[price] for the six weeks.

I don't know anyone. Will I be the odd one out?

Every cohort starts together on Week 1, so there's no existing group to break into.

What am I signing up for?

Six Wednesday evenings. After Week 6 it ends. If you'd like another cohort, you choose to join one.

I haven't got much time to read.

You're in good company: half of Irish adults surveyed say lack of time stops them reading more (RTÉ, Feb 2026). A fixed Wednesday gives your reading a place in the week.

Is it all literary fiction?

No. There are three cohorts: Page-turners (crime, thriller, mystery), Big worlds (sci-fi, fantasy, romantasy) and Real life (narrative non-fiction, essays, memoir). The questions below will point you to one.
